# Team Systems Sprint

## Formation Coordinator

FOLLOW, CAVE, COMBAT, and RETREAT layouts calculate role slots at a bounded cadence. Slots avoid the player, interaction blocks, unsafe terrain, unloaded chunks, and player-marked danger/protected/machine zones.

## Event Catalog and Team Director

The queue handles P0–P4 events with merge and expiry behavior. P0 emergencies can pre-empt lower work. Retreat is emergency-capable; Defend and Structure Entry are explicit player-approved plans.

## Exploration

Environment risk uses local measurable signals only: time, weather, light, nearby hostiles, lava, player health, and safe-ground availability. Structure inspection is restricted to a bounded scan around the actual server-side look target.

## Base, time, and recovery

Base Activity uses anchors without changing the world. First-home/current-home history, in-game day milestones, quiet windows, session recap, and Story Downed fallback are persisted in Team Blackboard data.

## Natural-world hostile interaction

The mod registers no hostile entities, custom enemy texture, natural spawn, or forced encounter content. Companion systems react to visible loaded Minecraft hostiles and compatible hostile mod mobs through server-owned safety gates. Will control, Guardian Brace, Scout Signal, Gifted powers, threat assessment, and formations stay bounded without replacing the normal survival world.
