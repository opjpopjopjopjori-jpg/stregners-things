# Professional Ambient Sound Quality Contract

Every sound selection uses professional audio engineering criteria:

- CINEMA_GRADE (intensity >= 0.85f): Full frequency response (20Hz-20kHz), spatial depth, emotional resonance, cinematic dynamics, zero clipping, immersive presence
- ACTION_SHARP (intensity >= 0.60f): Precise impact, quick decay (< 200ms), clear distinction between events, sharp attack, minimal overlap
- AMBIENT_DEEP (intensity <= 0.60f, > 0.35f): Layered atmosphere, subtle dynamics (3-6dB variation), immersive presence, seamless looping, no frequency masking
- EMOTIONAL_SOFT (intensity <= 0.35f): Gentle dynamics (< 3dB variation), warm tones (boosted 2-5kHz), minimal disruption, slow attack, extended decay

Every critical event (blizzard_critical, dragon_fire, rescue_emergency, etc.) triggers the appropriate quality level automatically based on context (chapter, emotional state, combat phase, namespace).
